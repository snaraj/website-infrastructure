### Fixed

- Bound Helm release-history retention with `spec.maxHistory: 2` on all three `HelmRelease` manifests — both site releases and the public connector release — and made that literal a required, value-pinned line of the reviewed release shape, so retention can no longer be omitted, dropped, or raised without failing the gate. Helm stores each revision as a Secret in the release namespace and prunes history to `maxHistory - 1` *before* writing the new revision, so an unset field, which retains five, can sit at or above a namespace Secret budget: the pre-create prune then frees nothing, the create is denied by quota, and no reconcile recovers it. A site release was measured wedged in that exact state at revision `v5` against a four-Secret budget, with a merged, signed, published chart it could not apply; the second site was measured holding four revisions, one upgrade short of the same wall. The connector release is bounded for a related but distinct reason: its namespace budget must also hold the reviewed SOPS tunnel credential for each connector, so retention is capped rather than left to consume the margin those Secrets need. Two revisions stay strictly inside every one of those budgets while preserving one rollback target for the `strategy: rollback` remediation each release already declares. Chart resolution, signature verification, and the promotion surface are untouched: the committed suspension gates and image pins are unchanged, and no digest, tag, or readiness scalar moves.
