### Fixed

- Bound Helm release-history retention on both site `HelmRelease` manifests with `spec.maxHistory: 2`, so a site's release history can no longer exhaust its namespace Secret budget and deadlock every future deploy. Helm stores each revision as a Secret and `storage.Create` prunes history to `maxHistory - 1` *before* writing the new revision, so helm-controller's unset five-revision default sat at or above the measured four-Secret namespace budget: the pre-create prune freed nothing, the create was denied by quota, and no reconcile could recover it. One site was measured wedged at revision `v5` with an `UpgradeFailed` release and a merged, signed, published chart that could not be applied; the other was measured holding four revisions, one upgrade short of the same wall. Two revisions keep steady-state history strictly inside the budget while preserving one rollback target for each release's existing `strategy: rollback` remediation. Chart resolution, signature verification, and the promotion surface are untouched — the committed suspension gates and image pins are unchanged, and no digest, tag, or readiness scalar moves.
